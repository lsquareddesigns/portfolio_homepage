02 · The solution

Meet Balanceo.

A habit app that holds your prepaid adventure until you finish the habits that prevent burnout. Three moments show how it works.

01

Decide while you’re motivated, not when you’re tired.

Book the reward first

  • Goals built around your real week
  • Discounted spa days, getaways and classes
  • The ticket locks at $0 until habits earn it back

Designed for↑ Follow-through  ·  ↑ Bookings

02

People know what helps. They just don’t do it.

Earn it back, one habit at a time

  • Slide a coin to log it, with a photo or timer as proof
  • Every habit earns real money toward the trip
  • Skipped habits pay more, so the trip stays winnable

Designed for↑ Daily check-ins  ·  ↑ Finished trips

03

Life happens.

Finish strong, or step away fairly

  • Keep going comes first, not the exit
  • Instant Unlock saves the ticket for $10
  • Encouraging copy beat “cheating” copy in an A/B test

Designed for↑ Trust  ·  + $10 unlock revenue
